# Digital Case Study Assessment (Assessment 2)

This assessment is worth **60% of the module**.

## What you produce

### 1. Digital Case Study (Video)
Create a **3-minute digital case study** explaining a **global bioscience challenge** to a **specific non-technical audience**.

Your video must:
- Use your **own voice** (no synthetic narration)
- Communicate a clear, structured message
- Follow a logical flow (hook → explain → solution → takeaway)
- Present an evidence-informed solution

You may use any platform (e.g. Canva, CapCut, PowerPoint, iMovie).  
**Do not subscribe** — free versions and watermarks are acceptable.  
Marks are awarded for **clarity and communication**, not polish.

You must **publish your case study to a public platform** (e.g. YouTube, LinkedIn, Vimeo).  
The link must be **fully accessible** and included in your reflection.

---

### 2. Reflective Commentary (500 words)

You must submit a **500-word reflection** that:
- Justifies your design decisions
- Defines and justifies your audience
- Critically evaluates your approach
- Uses relevant literature (Harvard referencing)

You must:
- Complete the **MachinaViva activity before writing**
- Append your **full MachinaViva transcript** (not included in word count)
- Include a **reference list** (not included in word count)

---

## AI Use

**Allowed:** planning, scripting support, visuals  
**Not allowed:** writing the reflection, fake justification, replacing understanding, synthetic voiceovers  

> Over-reliance on AI is obvious in reflection and AutoViva.

---

## Marking

- Digital artefact (video): **80%**  
- Reflective commentary: **20%**  

**Deadline:** Week 13 — 15th May

> If you can explain your idea clearly to a non-expert, you are on the right track.
